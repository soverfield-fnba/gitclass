The Great Gatsby follows the story of Nick Carraway as he gets entangled with his mysterious, wealthy neighbor Jay Gatsby and his cousin, Daisy Buchanan. Gatsby's elaborate parties are his attempt to win back Daisy, but their rekindled affair leads to a confrontation with her husband, Tom, and a tragic accident that results in Myrtle Wilson's death. Tom directs Myrtle's grieving husband, George, to Gatsby, who then murders Gatsby before killing himself. Disgusted by the moral emptiness of the wealthy elite, Nick returns to the Midwest, reflecting on the corrupted American Dream
